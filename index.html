About

From theoretical physics to mathematical models for real decision systems.

I started my academic journey in physics, with a particular interest in statistical mechanics, complex systems and mathematical modelling. Over time, my work moved toward optimization, online algorithms and learning-based decision-making. During my PhD, I have focused on problems where decisions must be made sequentially, with incomplete information and operational constraints — from online matching to resource allocation and human–AI service systems.
